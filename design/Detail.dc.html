Brian Michael Bendis and Mark Bagley’s ground-up retelling of Peter Parker, collected in oversized hardcover. Runs from the first radioactive spider through the Clone Saga, gathering issues #1–39 alongside the original scripts and Bagley’s pencils.
What is an omnibus? One oversized book collecting a long run of issues — the whole story in a single volume, rather than several thin trade paperbacks.
